Privacy policy

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Your browser can keep a shopping cart in its own local storage for when online checkout opens. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, to keep its service secure. That is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

Instagram and orders

Links to Instagram open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy. If you order, you share details such as your name and delivery address with Trill in an Instagram direct message. They are used to arrange and deliver your order.

Payments

This website has no payment form and never sees or stores your card number. Payment for an order is arranged with Trill.

Questions

Message Trill on Instagram with any question about your information.
