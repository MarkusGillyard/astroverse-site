About Astroverse

Reach for the star.

Our logo shows a galactic astronaut soaring through space to attain a star light years away. It stands for ambition, exploration and chasing goals that seem out of reach.

“This line was built to show my true perspective of myself. Welcome to Astroverse!”

Trill

What Astroverse means

Astroverse exists to inspire. It reaches out to creative designers and artists with a passion to be different, while expressing the art within.

Clothing is one way to make that expression tangible. That is the idea behind everything we make.

The journey so far

  1. 2022The first Astroverse drops: t-shirts and shorts for men and women, ordered by message.
  2. 2023A restock in every color, then limited-edition bucket hats and ski masks.
  3. 2026The Halloween Special Event opened on October 1, a fresh drop of printed tees followed on October 17, and Astroverse Merchandise got its own website.
