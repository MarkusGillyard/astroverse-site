FAQ

How do I order?

Send Trill a direct message on Instagram at @trillfam_lee with the design, color and size you want. Trill confirms the price and delivery with you there. There is no checkout on this website.

What will you sell?

Shirts, sweatpants, sweaters and bucket hats. Three shirt designs and one hoodie are revealed so far; sweatpants and bucket hats are coming soon.

Which designs have been revealed?

The Signature astronaut tee in white and black; two Halloween Limited Edition tees, Green Orbit in white and black and Haunted Orbit in orange and black; and the Black and Gold Astroverse Hoodie, listed under sweaters. Each is shown front and back.

How much will items cost?

Starting prices are $20 for shirts, $30 for sweatpants, $35 for sweaters and $15 for bucket hats. These are category starting prices; the price of each individual design has not been announced.

Which sizes are available?

The shirts and the hoodie come in Small, Medium and Large.

Where are new drops announced?

On Trill's Instagram, @trillfam_lee. The designs you can order are listed on this website.

Is my payment information safe?

This website has no payment form and never sees or stores your card number. Payment is arranged directly with Trill.
