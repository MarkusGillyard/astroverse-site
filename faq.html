FAQ

Can I order online yet?

Not yet. The storefront is being prepared, and ordering opens once payments, shipping and taxes are set up.

What will you sell?

Shirts, sweatpants, sweaters and bucket hats. Individual designs, sizes and colors will be announced as they are released.

How much will items cost?

Starting prices are $20 for shirts, $30 for sweatpants, $35 for sweaters and $15 for bucket hats.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments will be handled by a secure, established payment provider. This site will never see or store your card number.
